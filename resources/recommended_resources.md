# Recommended Resources for AI/ML Engineers

A hand-picked collection of the highest-signal courses, textbooks, foundational research papers, and open-source repositories to accelerate your journey from fresher to production AI engineer.

---

## 1. Essential Books & Textbooks
- **[Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and TensorFlow](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/hands-on-machine-learning/9781098125967/)** by Aurélien Géron — The ultimate practical guide for classical ML and deep learning implementation in Python.
- **[Designing Machine Learning Systems](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/designing-machine-learning/9781098107956/)** by Chip Huyen — The gold standard for understanding production MLOps, data drift, feature stores, and ML system design.
- **[Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/)** by Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville — The definitive mathematical and theoretical bible of deep learning.

---

## 2. Top Video Lectures & YouTube Channels
- **[Andrej Karpathy: Neural Networks: Zero to Hero](https://karpathy.ai/zero-to-hero.html)** — Build micrograd, makemore, and a complete GPT from scratch in pure Python and PyTorch. Mandatory viewing for deep learning intuition.
- **[3Blue1Brown: Essence of Linear Algebra & Neural Networks](https://www.3blue1brown.com/)** — Unmatched geometric visualization of vector spaces, matrices, eigenvalues, and backpropagation.
- **[StatQuest with Josh Starmer](https://statquest.org/)** — Clear, bite-sized visual explanations of every classical ML algorithm (Decision Trees, PCA, SVMs, Gradient Boosting).
- **[Fast.ai: Practical Deep Learning for Coders](https://course.fast.ai/)** by Jeremy Howard — A top-down, code-first approach to training and fine-tuning computer vision and NLP models.

---

## 3. Seminal Research Papers Every AI Engineer Must Read
Understanding the original papers separates top engineers from superficial tutorial copy-pasters:
1. **The Transformer Architecture:** *[Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)* (Vaswani et al., 2017)
2. **Residual Networks:** *[Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03385)* (He et al., 2015)
3. **Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning:** *[LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685)* (Hu et al., 2021)
4. **Agentic Reasoning & Tools:** *[ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629)* (Yao et al., 2022)
5. **Retrieval-Augmented Generation:** *[Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401)* (Lewis et al., 2020)

---

## 4. High-Signal Blogs & Technical Communities
- **[Lilian Weng's Blog (Lil'Log)](https://lilianweng.github.io/)** — Deep technical breakdowns of LLMs, Prompt Engineering, Autonomous Agents, and Diffusion models.
- **[Hugging Face Courses & Documentation](https://huggingface.co/learn)** — Practical, up-to-date guides for transformers, tokenizers, and parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT).
- **[Made With ML](https://madewithml.com/)** by Goku Mohandas — End-to-end MLOps curriculum covering Git, Docker, testing, tracking, and serving.

---

## 5. Foundational Open-Source Repositories to Explore
- **[vLLM](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm)** — High-throughput, memory-efficient LLM serving engine (PagedAttention).
- **[LangGraph](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langgraph)** — Multi-agent state machine and cyclical graph orchestration.
- **[Ollama](https://github.com/ollama/ollama)** — Run open-weight models (Llama 3, Mistral, DeepSeek) locally with zero hassle.
